### Key reusable HUD observations (Turf War)
- Top-left: stopwatch icon + M:SS timer (3:00 match); turns yellow under 1:00; large translucent numeral countdown in the final seconds (seen "2" at 0:02).
- Top-centre: 4 vs 4 squid-head icons in team colours; splatted players shown as black icons with X eyes; "Danger!" badge sometimes attached near enemy side.
- Top-right: 4-digit personal turf points counter "p"; circular special gauge ring around a humanoid icon, "Press R" + "Charged!" when full; three gear-ability icons below.
- Bottom-left: D-pad callouts "C'mon!" / "Booyah!".
- Centre: small pink circle reticle. Ink tank = world-space vertical battery gauge beside the character (appears in squid form/refill), plus "Low ink!" text under reticle when empty. No permanent ink bar.
- Death: black spiky banner "Splatted/Squished by <weapon/special>!", killer's gear panel, "Respawn in N" bottom-right, enemy-ink splatter framing screen.
- Ally special activation: name + portrait callout box at right ("Kraken").
- Results: overhead painted map + judge + "Good Guys % / p" vs "Bad Guys % / p"; then per-player scoreboard with level, weapon icon, points, splats/deaths, VICTORY/DEFEAT groups.
